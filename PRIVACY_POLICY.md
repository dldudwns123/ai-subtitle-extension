# Privacy Policy for AI Real-Time Phonetic & Translation Subtitles

**Last Updated:** 2026.11.10

## 1. Information We Collect
We do not collect any personally identifiable information (such as names, email addresses, or payment details). 
The extension only accesses the current YouTube video URL and its audio content to generate real-time subtitles and translations.

## 2. How We Use Information
The YouTube URL and audio data are transmitted to our backend server solely for AI processing (transcription and translation). All temporary audio files created during processing are immediately deleted from our server.

## 3. Data Storage & Sharing
- We do not permanently store user data, audio files, or viewing history.
- We do not sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties.

## 4. Contact
For any privacy concerns or questions regarding this extension, please contact us via GitHub Issues.
